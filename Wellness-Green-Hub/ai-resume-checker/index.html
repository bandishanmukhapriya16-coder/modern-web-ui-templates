WHY IT MATTERS

Your resume is your first conversation.

Recruiters often review applications quickly. A clear structure, relevant skills, and measurable achievements can make your experience easier to understand.

01

Keep your strongest achievements visible.

02

Match keywords to the role you're applying for.

03

Use concise, action-focused descriptions.

RESUME TIPS

Small changes. Better impact.

01

Use action verbs

Start achievement statements with words like built, designed, analyzed, improved, or developed.

02

Show measurable impact

Whenever possible, explain your result using numbers, percentages, time saved, or growth.

03

Keep it focused

Prioritize experience and skills that are relevant to the position you want.
